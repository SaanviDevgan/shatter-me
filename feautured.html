Character Files

Five characters. Five different stories. One unforgettable universe.

05 CHARACTER FILES FOUND ♡
NO. 001

Juliette Ferrars

The Protagonist

A girl who has spent much of her life isolated from the world. Her journey is about discovering her own strength and deciding who she wants to be.

Little fact: Her touch has a dangerous ability.

NO. 002

Aaron Warner

The Commander

Ambitious, composed and difficult to read. Warner has a polished exterior, but there is much more to his personality than first appears.

Little fact: He has a notable interest in fashion.

NO. 003

Nazeera Ibrahim

The Independent One

Clever, independent and often hard to figure out. Nazeera has connections to powerful families and a personality that keeps people guessing.

Little fact: She is not someone who easily follows others.

NO. 004

Kenji Kishimoto

The Best Friend

Witty, energetic and full of personality. Kenji brings humour to difficult situations while remaining a fiercely loyal friend.

Little fact: His humour is a big part of his personality.

NO. 005

Adam Kent

The Soldier

A determined soldier with a complicated past. Adam's story explores loyalty, family and the difficult choices people make.

Little fact: He is James's older brother.
